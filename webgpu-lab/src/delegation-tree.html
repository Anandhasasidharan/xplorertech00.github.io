Delegation Tree — authority can only narrow

Delegation may narrow authority, never expand it.

A human operator hands a scope to a manager agent, the manager hands a smaller scope to a worker, and the worker hands a single-file scope to a tool. Each child scope is drawn inside its parent's scope and shrinks at every hop. Try to widen a scope and the delegation is refused before any token is issued. This is the attenuation guarantee from AgentIAM — invocation-bound capability tokens where the delegation chain is the audit trail.
